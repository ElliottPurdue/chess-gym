    Move progress between copies

    Copy your progress here, then paste it into the other copy of the Gym (the offline one at localhost, or the online one on claude.ai). Pasting adds to what is already there: no puzzle reviews, XP or streak days are lost, and the pasted copy's ratings and session win.

    Good to know

    This is the offline copy running on your PC. Progress here is saved separately from your online Chess Gym, and Sunday's new positions only reach the online one.

    The Chess Coach overlay (the Chrome extension from our chat) runs on Chess.com: it tracks your rapid results and locks for review after a loss. The weekly review runs Sunday evenings and adds new positions here.

    Right-click a square to mark it red, right-drag to draw an arrow, left-click to clear. Right-click while dragging a piece cancels the move. Drag the corner to resize the board.
